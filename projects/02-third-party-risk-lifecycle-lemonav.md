# Third-Party Risk Lifecycle Process Design

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Client** | Lemonav Labs |
| **Date** | 2025–2026 |
| **Type** | Process / Governance |
| **Framework** | Internal GRC Policy (TP-01, TP-02) |

---

## Overview

Designed and documented a formal Third-Party Risk Lifecycle workflow for Lemonav Labs. The goal was to replace ad hoc vendor intake practices with a repeatable, documented process covering vendor onboarding, security review, approval/denial decisions, and ongoing annual reassessment.

---

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Process Stages Defined | 5 |
| Scope | All vendors |
| Review Cadence | Annual |
| Decision Gates | 2 (Approve / Deny) |

---

## Process Stages

```
Request Received
       ↓
Initial Assessment
       ↓
Security Review
       ↓
Address Questions / Gaps
       ↓
Vendor Approval / Denial
       ↓
Annual Review
```

---

## Key Deliverable

- **Visual process flow diagram** mapping the complete vendor risk lifecycle from intake to ongoing oversight
- Documented escalation paths for borderline or high-risk vendors
- Decision criteria defined at each gate to support consistent, auditable outcomes

---

## Outcome

Standardized vendor onboarding and risk governance process with:
- Clearly defined roles and responsibilities at each stage
- Documented escalation paths
- Annual reassessment trigger built into the lifecycle
- Process aligned to internal TP-01 and TP-02 policy requirements

---

## Frameworks Applied
- Internal GRC Policy: TP-01 (Vendor Review Requirements)
- Internal GRC Policy: TP-02 (Ongoing Vendor Oversight)
