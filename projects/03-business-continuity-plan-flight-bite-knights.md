## Overview

Developed a comprehensive Business Continuity Plan (BCP) for a drone-based food delivery startup. The plan addressed both physical and cyber risk scenarios unique to drone operations, including GPS spoofing, drone hijacking, cloud-based DDoS attacks, and FAA regulatory non-compliance.
