# Risk Assessment | Credit Card Processing Implementation

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Client** | Kora's Customs (Costume Design) |
| **Date** | November 2025 |
| **Type** | Risk Assessment |
| **Framework** | PCI DSS; Internal Risk Register (R-001 through R-012) |

---

## Overview

Conducted a risk assessment for a small costume design business expanding into a warehouse operation and implementing credit card payment processing for the first time. The assessment identified 12 risks and produced a phased remediation roadmap tailored to a small business budget.

---

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Total Risks Identified | 12 |
| Critical Risks | 4 |
| High Risks | 5 |
| Medium Risks | 3 |
| Recommended Budget | $15,000–$25,000 (6 months) |

---

## Top Critical Risks

All four critical risks received a **risk score of 25** (maximum):

| Risk ID | Risk | Score |
|---|---|---|
| R-001 | Customer Payment Data Breach | 25 |
| R-002 | PCI DSS Non-Compliance | 25 |
| R-003 | Inadequate Employee Security Training | 25 |
| R-004 | No Cyber Insurance Coverage | 25 |

---

## Potential Exposure

| Exposure Type | Estimated Range |
|---|---|
| PCI DSS Non-Compliance Fines | $5,000–$100,000/month |
| Average Breach Cost (Small Business) | $150,000+ |

---

## Remediation Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Weeks 1–2 | Cost: $3,000–$5,000
- Obtain cyber insurance policy
- Set up compliant payment processor
- Deploy initial security tooling

### Phase 2 — Weeks 3–10
- Employee security awareness training
- Access control implementation
- Network segmentation for payment systems

### Phase 3 — Months 4–6
- Full PCI DSS gap remediation
- Policy documentation
- Ongoing monitoring and review

---

## Outcome

Delivered a prioritized risk register (R-001 through R-012) and a phased remediation roadmap. The recommended investment pays for itself by preventing even a single security incident.
