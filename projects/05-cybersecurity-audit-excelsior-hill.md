# Cybersecurity Audit | NIST CSF v1.1

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Client** | Excelsior Hill University |
| **Date** | April 29, 2025 |
| **Type** | Cybersecurity Audit |
| **Framework** | NIST CSF v1.1; NCSR Maturity Level Scoring (1–7 scale) |

---

## Overview

Evaluated Excelsior Hill University's Information Security Policy against all **108 subcategory controls** in the NIST CSF v1.1. Each of the five control functions was scored using the NCSR (Nationwide Cybersecurity Review) maturity methodology on a 1–7 scale.

---

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Controls Evaluated | 108 |
| Average Maturity Score | 5.8 / 7 |
| Gaps Identified | 3 |
| Lowest Scoring Function | Identify (5/7) |
| Highest Scoring Functions | Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover (6/7 each) |

---

## Gaps Identified

### Gap 1 — ID.BE-1
**Finding:** The organization's role in the supply chain is not clearly identified or communicated.  
**Function:** Identify — Business Environment

### Gap 2 — ID.BE-2
**Finding:** The organization's place in critical infrastructure and industry sector is not formally documented.  
**Function:** Identify — Business Environment

### Gap 3 — ID.RA-2
**Finding:** No formal process exists to receive and incorporate cyber threat intelligence from external information sharing forums (e.g., ISACs).  
**Function:** Identify — Risk Assessment

---

## Strengths

- **Incident Response Policy** — Cited as impressive; well-structured and actionable
- **Contingency Planning** — Updated annually; demonstrates strong operational discipline

---

## Outcome

Delivered a full audit report with maturity scores across all 108 NIST CSF controls, three documented gaps with remediation recommendations, and identified institutional strengths to preserve.
