## Overview

Completed a full Data Privacy Impact Assessment (DPIA) for Flight Bite Knights' drone-based food delivery application. The assessment documented data flows, established a legal basis for processing, assessed privacy risks, reviewed implemented security controls, and obtained formal sign-off from required stakeholders.

---

## Data Types Processed

- Customer names and addresses
- GPS location data (real-time and historical)
- Order history
- Payment details

---

## Privacy Risks Assessed

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood |
|---|---|---|
| Data breach of cloud storage | Critical | Unlikely |
| Unauthorized GPS data access | High | Possible |
| Payment data exposure | High | Unlikely |

---

## Controls Implemented

| Control | Detail |
|---|---|
| GPS Data Anonymization | GPS data anonymized post-delivery; not retained in identifiable form |
| Encryption | End-to-end encryption for data in transit and at rest |
| Penetration Testing | Regular third-party pen testing of application and infrastructure |
| Access Controls | Role-based access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel |

---

## Outcome

DPIA formally approved with a recommendation for annual risk reassessment. Signed off by the CEO, DPO, and Product Manager. Next review scheduled for July 2026.

---

## Frameworks Applied
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- Privacy by Design principles
