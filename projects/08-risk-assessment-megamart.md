## Overview

Conducted an enterprise-wide risk assessment for a 500+ location retail corporation using NIST SP 800-30 and SP 800-171 frameworks. Assessed 25 risks across all three tiers of the NIST risk framework and produced a phased 18-month remediation roadmap with investment planning.

## Top Risks Identified

### High Risk #1 — Supply Chain Security Vulnerabilities
- **Likelihood:** Moderate
- **Impact:** High
- Risks stemming from third-party supplier relationships and lack of supply chain visibility across 500+ locations

### High Risk #2 — Distributed Location Security Monitoring
- Inconsistent security monitoring and control implementation across 500+ retail sites
- Limited centralized visibility into endpoint and network activity
