## Overview

Developed a comprehensive governance policy for a small coffee shop business. The policy covers all core operational and security governance areas, written in plain language accessible to non-technical staff without a dedicated IT or compliance function.

## Policy Areas Covered

- Cash register and point-of-sale access control
- Password security requirements
- Employee hiring and background screening process
- Large purchase approval workflows
- Separation of duties (cash handling, reconciliation)
- Bank deposit procedures
- Third-party vendor management
- Computer and device access
- Financial records and retention

---

## Key Policy KPIs

| Policy Control | Standard |
|---|---|
| Minimum Password Length | 8 characters with complexity requirements |
| Password Rotation | Mandatory every 90 days |
| Terminated Employee Access Revocation | Within 24 hours of separation |

---

## Enforcement Structure

| Tier | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Written Warning |
| Tier 2 | Suspension |
| Tier 3 | Termination |

---

## Outcome

Delivered a practical, readable governance policy tailored for a small retail business. The policy gives Jabba Java a defensible governance framework without requiring dedicated compliance or IT personnel to administer.
