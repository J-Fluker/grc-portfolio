## Overview

Created a Code of Conduct and Compliance Policy for a consumer-facing children's clothing brand. The policy addresses legal compliance, family-friendly product standards, employee conduct, vendor screening, and incident reporting — with specific attention to the company's obligations as a brand serving children and families.

| Pillar | Description |
