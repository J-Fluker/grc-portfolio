## Key Findings

### Control Weaknesses Identified
- **Password Reset Procedures** — Inconsistent processes create risk of unauthorized account recovery
- **User Guidance** — Insufficient user-facing documentation on secure authentication practices
- **Temporary Password Reuse** — Temporary passwords being reused, violating PCI-DSS requirements

### Remediation Recommendations
- Eliminate reused temporary passwords; enforce single-use temporary credentials
- Increase minimum password length to meet PCI-DSS v4.0 standards
- Standardize helpdesk password reset procedures with documented, auditable workflow
