# Cloud Security Risk Assessment | Cloud Halo

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Client** | Cloud Halo |
| **Date** | 2025 |
| **Type** | Risk Assessment |

---

## Overview

Performed a cloud security risk assessment to identify technical and procedural risks impacting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA triad) of cloud-hosted systems. Evaluated risks across access control, data protection, and operational security processes.

---

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Cloud Services Reviewed | 100% of in-scope |
| Risk Attributes Assigned | Likelihood, Impact, Risk Rating, Mitigation |
| Scope | Access control, data protection, operational security |

---

## Assessment Scope

All identified risks were documented in a structured risk register and assigned:

- **Likelihood** rating
- **Impact** rating
- **Overall Risk Rating** (combination of likelihood × impact)
- **Recommended Mitigation** action

---

## Focus Areas

| Domain | Assessment Coverage |
|---|---|
| Access Control | IAM configurations, privilege management, authentication controls |
| Data Protection | Encryption at rest and in transit, data classification, retention |
| Operational Security | Patch management, logging and monitoring, incident response readiness |

---

## Deliverables

- Cloud security risk assessment report
- Risk register with all identified risks rated and documented
- Control recommendations and prioritized remediation actions

---

## Outcome

- Established a structured cloud risk baseline for the organization
- Provided prioritized remediation guidance for high-impact risks
- Improved leadership visibility into cloud-specific control gaps and operational exposure
- Built a defensible cloud risk assessment aligned with enterprise risk management practices
