# Third-Party Risk & Vendor Tracking Program

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Client** | Internal |
| **Date** | 2025 |
| **Type** | Vendor / Third-Party Risk Management |

---

## Overview

Designed and maintained a vendor tracking and third-party risk management workbook to support ongoing vendor oversight, risk visibility, and audit readiness. Replaced fragmented vendor documentation with a centralized, structured tracking system aligned to GRC workflows.

---

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Vendors Captured | 100% of active vendors |
| Tracking Format | Centralized spreadsheet (XLSX) |
| Fields Per Vendor Record | Risk status, assessment status, review dates, ownership |
| Review Cadence Tracking | Enabled for all vendors |

---

## Vendor Record Fields

Each vendor record in the tracking register includes:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Vendor Name | Identification |
| Risk Status | Current risk rating (Low / Medium / High) |
| Assessment Status | Assessed / Pending / Due for Renewal |
| Last Review Date | Date of most recent formal assessment |
| Next Review Date | Trigger for upcoming reassessment |
| Risk Owner | Named individual responsible for vendor oversight |

- Vendor risk status and review tracking system
- Risk ownership and review cadence documentation
